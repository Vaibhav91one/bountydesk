Prototype

Every mobile screen on iOS and Android, side by side and clickable, with each platform's motion. Tap in either phone and both follow. Haptics show as tags, and the gesture buttons play back swipe, sheet dismissal, pull to refresh, long press and a push notification. Switch on light theme, large text, a screen reader view or a touch target check. Scroll to go through the screens in journey order, or tap buttons, rows, tabs and back buttons to follow the flow.

Access
Gestures
Scroll, or use the arrow keys, to go through every screen
